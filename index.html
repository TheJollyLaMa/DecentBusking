🏆 The community makes the chart

👍 Every vote moves the music

Songs rank by this week’s net votes: upvotes minus downvotes. Use the radio’s thumbs while a song plays. The chart updates live; each song holds its own place, even when one artist has several hits.

💸 A weekly prize ladder

First place receives half the available weekly Top 10 pot. Each next place receives half the remainder, and the final payable place gets the rest. Every payable prize must exceed $0.10 USDC; a smaller pot supports fewer prizes.

Shown prizes are estimates, not guaranteed payments. Rankings and estimates freeze at Monday midnight in America/New_York. Artist payout wallets must be linked, and payments require owner approval.

🎉 Friday Top 10 Hype

When the scheduled Hype hour begins, its playlist snapshots the opening Top 10 and counts up from #10 toward #1. Votes keep moving the live chart while those songs play. The festival panel opens for the show; you can still minimize it.

➕ Keep a favorite

Use + to choose your current draft, a saved named playlist, or ➕ New playlist… (name it inline; 24 songs maximum). Use 👀 to save the song and artist to your profile watchlist. Saved playlist additions publish a new immutable revision; saved copies become your own original. Saving does not buy playback.
