🎧 Booth setup · required before your mic starts
  1. Plug your headphones into your audio interface, not the computer.
  2. Turn the interface's direct monitor on. You hear yourself instantly, with no delay.
  3. Put effects in your interface or mixer, before the browser, so listeners hear them too.
  4. Turn the radio off on every other tab, phone and speaker. It runs seconds behind you and sounds doubled.

Quick soundcheck fixes
  • Hear yourself through your interface: headphones in the interface, direct monitor on, Hear my input off.
  • Hearing yourself twice or late? The radio or browser monitor is playing somewhere. Turn it off; your mic keeps sending.
  • Headphones won't fit the interface? Use a 3.5 mm to ¼-inch stereo adapter.
  • If no input appears, allow microphone access in browser site settings, then reload and choose your mic or interface.
  • If the meter is quiet, check the interface input, cable and gain. The local meter is not proof of room delivery.
